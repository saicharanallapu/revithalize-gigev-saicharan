About and methodology

GigEV by Revitalize Mobility shows riders and fleet owners whether going electric pays.

How we calculate

Monthly km = daily km x 26 working days.
Petrol cost per km = petrol price / mileage, or your stated daily spend.
Fixed battery cost per km = tariff x (Wh/km / 1000).
Swap cost = km / 96 x Rs. 40 (48V 44Ah battery).
Savings = petrol cost minus electric cost.
Payback = retrofit cost (Rs. 35,000) / monthly savings.
CO2 = petrol litres x 2.31 kg minus kWh x 0.71 kg.

All maths runs in code, not in an AI model.

Sources and limits

PM e-DRIVE terms come from the Ministry of Heavy Industries notification reported in Aug 2026. The Telangana waiver comes from the state government order. Fuel price, tariff, Wh/km and mileage are editable assumptions. Fleet figures are demo data. Whether a retrofitted bike qualifies for subsidies is unconfirmed.
